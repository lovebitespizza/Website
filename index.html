Our Story

Three generations, one oven.

Bella Fetta started as a single brick oven in Nonna Rosa's backyard. Today, that same oven — rebuilt brick by brick — still bakes every pie we serve. We use only imported Italian flour, San Marzano tomatoes, and mozzarella made fresh each morning.

No freezers. No shortcuts. Just the recipe our family has trusted for over sixty years.
