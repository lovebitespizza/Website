Our Vibe

Pizza with a little romance.

Love Bites Pizza is Winnipeg's spot for hand-tossed pies, crispy "Heartbreaker" wings, and fryer favorites built for sharing (or not — we don't judge). Every pizza, side, and combo on our menu comes with its own love language, from the classic "Plain Jane" to "The Toxic Relationship" combo.

Open every day from 11am to 2am, because cravings don't keep business hours.
